# Lessons

* Keep product claims distinct from public-sector context and recommended measures.
* For RFP and RFI-adjacent writing, avoid hyperbole and absolute commitments.
* Use agency-neutral examples unless the user explicitly authorizes client references.
* For campaign-finance products, lead with filer registration, structured financial transactions, disclosure reports, filing calendars, validation, amendments, audits, termination, and public transparency.
* Avoid licensing and permitting concepts such as generic applications, issuance, renewals, credentials, inspections, and field work unless the source establishes a campaign-finance-specific use.
